Firmware Release Notes

The MUGIC is a wireless motion sensor that streams your movement — orientation, rotation, and acceleration — into music and creative software in real time.

Version 2.1.4

July 2026 New update
  • A small follow-up to 2.1.3 that minimizes streaming interruptions while actively changing settings over Bluetooth or the web page.
  • Previously, to limit interactions, streaming was interrupted while a BLE or HTTP config page was open (even if idle).
  • Now, streaming is suspended only briefly (at most two seconds) while interacting with the config page, but not if the page is idle. Streaming remains suspended during OTA updates.

Version 2.1.3

July 2026

The first firmware update since launch. It makes the MUGIC more stable and dependable, and gives you more control over the motion data you send.

More reliable, everywhere

  • Fewer crashes and freezes. Fixed issues that could cause the device to hang, restart, or get stuck in a reboot loop — including during updates.
  • Steadier streaming. Motion streaming now pauses cleanly while you change settings, then resumes on its own.
  • Dependable activity logs. Logs save reliably, download in full for troubleshooting, and clear with a single tap.

More control over your motion data

  • Choose how acceleration is measured. Switch between linear acceleration (your movement only, with gravity filtered out) and raw acceleration (total force, including gravity) — whichever suits your instrument or software.
  • Custom data labels. Give your motion stream its own name so multiple MUGICs or channels are easy to tell apart in your software.

Easier setup

  • Smarter Bluetooth. Bluetooth turns on only when you need it and switches off when idle, so Wi-Fi streaming and battery life aren't affected.
  • Clearer configuration screen, with improved Wi-Fi labeling and a cleaner layout.

Version 2.0.1

October 2024 Originally shipped with your device

The firmware your MUGIC came with when you bought it. Streams motion over Wi-Fi (in the standard OSC format music apps understand) or USB, sends to multiple destinations at once, supports Bluetooth setup, full-color LED status, rechargeable-battery monitoring, and wireless updates.
