Firmware Update

Update your MUGIC® device over the USB-C cable, right from your browser — no terminal or install needed. Connect the device, choose a firmware file, and click Update.

Curious what changed? See the firmware release notes.

For the occasional upgrade, an over-the-air (OTA) update is the easier way and should be your first choice. Use this page instead when:

  • OTA is failing or unavailable, or
  • you need to flash several devices in a row.

Before you start:

  • Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (Safari and Firefox are not supported). If no device appears when you click Connect, install the free CH34x USB driver (macOS 13+ usually has it built in; Windows normally needs it once), then reconnect.
  • Use a USB-C data cable — one that can sync a phone or transfer files. The cable that came with your MUGIC is power-only and will not work for flashing.

Then, on the right:

  • Connect the device — the installed firmware version is shown automatically.
  • Choose the main.bin firmware file (from your computer, or the one included with this page).
  • Click Update firmware and wait — do not unplug until it finishes. The device restarts into the new firmware.
