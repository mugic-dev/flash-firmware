Firmware Flasher

Update your MUGIC device over the USB-C cable — no terminal needed.

Before you start: Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (Safari and Firefox are not supported). Plug the device in with a USB-C data cable (not a charge-only cable). If no device appears when you click Connect, install the free CH34x USB driver (macOS 13+ usually has it built in; Windows normally needs it once), then reconnect.

Advanced: full flash (rarely needed)
